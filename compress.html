iPhone: tap Save, then choose Save to Files (or Save Image / Save Video to put it in Photos).

Photos and videos are re-encoded at lower quality and size. PDFs are rebuilt from page images, so text can no longer be selected. ZIPs and other files are only recompressed losslessly, so already-compressed files may not reach the target. Videos are processed on your device and can be slow on phones, so keep them under about 300 MB.
